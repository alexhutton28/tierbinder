# Tier list

Part of TierBinder's notes, indexed in `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them matching the code in the same PR that changes it.

- 1 to 8 tiers (`MIN_TIERS`, `MAX_TIERS`). New tier lists start with 4: Goated, Solid, Mid, Fraud.
- The board keeps its size, so the tiers share its height (`tierHeight`). Up to 4 tiers hold full-size cards (fewer just have taller rows); past that, cards shrink with the rows (`tierCardWidth`: 80px wide at 5 tiers, 50px at 8), their gap in step. It's the type's `cardWidth`, so cards dragged to and from the card tray resize to it.
- Customize's Tiers section adds and removes them: each tier's row ends in a remove button (a `quiet` icon button with Feather's x, disabled on the last tier), and Add Tier (a `text` button, Feather's plus before its words, disabled at 8) follows the rows; the section holds nothing else. A new tier goes at the foot in the first of the tiers' eight colors not in use (`addTier`), and its name is focused and selected to type over (not on touch screens). While no tier has been renamed (each still has its place's word or letter: `unnamed`), adding one gives them all the default names of a list that long (`defaultTierStyle`): the four words up to 4 tiers, and letters past that, so a fifth tier turns Goated, Solid, Mid, Fraud into S, A, B, C, D, and the next is E (then F, G). Once any is renamed, the others keep their names and the new one is "Tier 5" (or the next number no tier is named for), or, when every name is still one of the letters (one was removed), the first letter not in use. Removing a tier never renames the rest. Reset Board Styles uses the same default names for the board's tier count, and never changes how many there are, nor does a clear. Removing a tier (`removeTier`) sends its cards to the end of the card tray, as many as fit, turning it on, with a toast offering Undo (a double click's second click is ignored, since the next tier's button moves under the pointer; "Tier Removed; Moved 3 Cards to the Card Tray", and a line for any that didn't fit and were removed); focus goes to the remove button that took its place, or to Add Tier when one tier is left. Settings show toasts through `onNotify` (`SettingsProps`).
- The Add to choice follows its tier as tiers come and go: targets carry an `id` (the tier's), and the editor moves the choice to that id's new place, or to the tier now in its place if it's gone.
- Cards start 20px after the label (`CARDS_PADDING`, `cardsLeft`), and a full row ends 20px before the board's edge. They have a 25px gap (in step with their size) that shrinks (negative overlaps, later cards on top) once a row is full; 8 fit at the default gap.
- A tier takes any number of cards: past what fits its row, they overlap further the more there are, the last still ending 20px before the board's edge. There's no limit and no "tier is full" message; an overcrowded tier is the maker's to fix.
- Label Width (`labelWidth`, a `Segmented` S, M, L, first in the Style section) sets how wide the tiers' labels are: one of `LABEL_WIDTHS` (70, 120 or 170px; 170, the widest, on a new list). Cards start 20px after the label, so a narrower label gives a narrow board's cards more room; names shrink to fit it (`fitLabel`). Reset Board Styles puts it back; a list saved before it is 170.
- Settings: `background`, `border` (Tier Dividers: the lines between tiers, and split card dividers and strokes) and `tierCardBorders` (Tier Card Borders: a 3px stroke inside each card in its tier's color).
- Tier labels are Inter Black at 30px, shrinking a pixel at a time down to 12px until the name fits its box (`fitLabel`): a word too long for it ("Charmander") or too many lines. Names wrap at spaces, never mid-word (Konva's own wrap splits words). A name can hold line breaks: Shift+Enter in its field (a textarea a line taller for each, which doesn't wrap; Enter alone does nothing).
- Dragging a card moves or reorders it (`dropTarget`, `moveCard`). A search result dropped beside a narrower board (the stage is as wide as the card tray) isn't added. A search result dropped on the board goes in the tier under the pointer, before the first card whose center is to its right.
- Clicking a tier's row anywhere but on a card (its label, the empty space) makes it the Add to tier, and with a card selected, clicking its empty space (not its label: `onPickTarget`'s `emptySpace`) also moves that card to the end of the tier, keeping it selected (`pickTargetOnBoard` in `Editor.tsx`, through the type's `moveCardTo`; a tray card is taken out of the tray and added there; one undo step). The Stage's `onMouseDown` deselects on a press of the background before the click, so the selection is read at `pointerdown` (`pressSelection`). A press on the card's own tier (its space or its label) or the tray it's in deselects the card instead, with a mouse or a finger (so tapping a card and then beside it doesn't reorder it). Nothing moves while a replacement is being picked, on another tier's label (which only picks that tier), on a double click's second press, or when the card is already last there (`moveCardTo` returns the list as it was). Clicking with nothing selected only picks the tier. From the keyboard, the card menu's Move to Tier and Alt and the arrows do the same (see Keyboard on the board). Cards are Konva groups named `card`, which is how the canvas tells them apart.
- Bulk import adds each `#` group to the next tier, after the cards already there, and renames the tier if the group has a name. Groups past the last tier add tiers, named after them, up to 8. Groups past the eighth tier are left out and reported; it never clears the board. Cards before the first `#` go in the card tray instead (`parseImport` returns `tray` and `groups`); one Import button does both.
- Double-clicking a tier label switches to the Customize tab and focuses that name.
