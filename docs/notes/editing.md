Part of TierBinder's notes, indexed in `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them matching the code in the same PR that changes it.

- Clicking or tapping a card selects it (`CardNode`'s `mousedown` and `tap`), and clicking or tapping it again deselects it (`onDeselect`: on the `click` or `tap` once it's over, since a press on a selected card may start its drag, after which Konva fires neither; not the tap that ends a long press); right-clicking it (the browser's own menu is prevented on cards only, opening it from `contextmenu`) or a long press (a touch or pen held `LONG_PRESS`, 500ms in `ui/pointer.ts`, moving less than `TOUCH_SLOP`; a touch or pen press drags only past `TOUCH_SLOP` too (`dragDistance`), so a held finger's wobble doesn't start one; cancelled by a drag starting, and Android's own contextmenu for it ignored; the menu opens where the finger went down, and closes if the finger then drags the card or pans the tray) opens the card menu, HTML over the board, on the board and in the card tray, in presentation mode too:
  - The menu stretches out to the right from its left edge (`.tierbinder-menu` in `globals.css`) on `--ease-spring`, and on close folds back to its left edge in 180ms, fading over the last part (`shownMenu` keeps the last menu on screen for `FOLD_CLEARANCE` (220ms, `ui/motion.ts`, shared by every closing popup), on a timer since `animationend` doesn't fire in a tab that isn't painting). It's keyed by card and position, so each opening replays the animation. A menu that would run past the window's foot is raised just enough to end 8px (`EDGE`) above it (`raisedMenu`), so it never makes the page scroll on a phone.
  - Card: View, Move to Tier (or Add to Tier, see below), Remove Card, Make Split Card (Replace Card was taken out for now; written in sentence case in code, since they are the menu's keys, and title-cased where shown: `titleCase` in `text.ts`).
  - Split card: View, Move to Tier, Remove Card, Replace Top Half, Replace Bottom Half, Swap Halves, Unsplit (keeps the top).
  - Move to Tier (a board card) or Add to Tier (a tray card: `moveLabel`), second while the card has somewhere to go, is how the keyboard moves a card to another tier. With only one place to go (a blank canvas card: the card tray) it's a plain item naming it, "Move to Card Tray" (`MOVE_ONE` and the place's index). Presentation mode (the sidebar folded on a phone: `presenting`): every card's menu is only View, Move to Tier and Remove Card, since replacing and splitting need the sidebar. That row (`MOVE_TO`) opens a flyout, as Clear Card Tray's does (sharing its `flyout` state, with `flyoutRow` saying which row's is open, since a tray card's menu has both: opening one closes the other), of every place the card can go with its swatch (`moveChoices`: not the tray or tier it's in, and on the blank canvas not the canvas for a board card); entering or leaving presentation mode closes an open card menu (`wasPresenting`), moving it there (`moveCardToTarget`, shared with clicking a tier), and moving up to stay inside the window.
  - A card in the card tray, split or not, has Clear Card Tray last, while the tray holds more than that card (see Card tray).
  - A search result dropped on a card opens the menu with the result's choices (`cardMenu.dropped`): Add as Split Card (the result is the bottom half) or Add to Tier/Canvas (the graphic's `dropCard` at the drop point); on a split card, Replace Top Half, Replace Bottom Half or Add to tier/canvas.
  - View (focused when it opens, as a modal dialog, and handing focus back when it closes) shows the card large in the middle of the window over a dimmed page (as tall as `--tb-view-height`, narrower to fit; a split card shows both cards side by side), growing from its place on the board on the spring and shrinking back to it when anything is pressed or Escape; it's modal: keys stop at it (so Delete can't remove the card behind it), Tab stays on it, and the sidebar column and workspace are `inert`; closing hands focus to the board (`boardBoxRef`, a Tab stop; its focus ring shows only with no card selected, since the outline shows where focus is), with the card still selected and its menu closed (`viewing`/`shownView` in `Editor.tsx`, kept on screen `FOLD_CLEARANCE` while it closes, like the menu).
  - Replace and Make split card switch to the Add Cards tab and put it in picking mode (`picking`: `{ id, part: 'top' | 'bottom' }`): replacing searches that card's name, and the next result clicked takes its place (or becomes the bottom half), until Cancel or Escape. Making a split card (the bottom half of an unsplit card) shows in the Add to picker instead of a message and Cancel, when it has more than one choice: a white card-shaped swatch (`SPLIT_SWATCH`) and "Splitting <name>" (`splitPicker`, Dropdown's `current`), with no option selected; picking any target cancels it. Screen readers hear it through a hidden status: "Splitting <name>: pick the bottom card from the results, or a tier to cancel."
- The Add Cards tab: clicking or tapping Add Cards in the main nav while it's already showing focuses the search (in the click, so a phone brings up its keyboard); switching to it from another tab doesn't, nor do the arrow keys (`RadioBar`'s `onChange` says whether it was `clicked`). While a search runs they're card-shaped placeholders with a dark shimmer (`SKELETON_TILES`), and each thumbnail shimmers until its image loads. Click one (or Enter) to add it to the chosen target, flying in from its thumbnail (see Flying in), or drag it onto the board (pointer events, not HTML drag and drop: a copy of the card follows the pointer after a 5px move, and the release goes to the graphic's `dropCard`; Escape cancels; mouse and pen only, since on touch a press-and-move scrolls the results). Right-clicking a result (or the menu key on it, or on touch a long press: held `LONG_PRESS`, 500ms, moving less than `TOUCH_SLOP`, 8px; the tap it ends with doesn't add the card, Android's own contextmenu for it is ignored, and iOS's image callout is off on results) opens its menu at the pointer (`resultMenu`; its rows are 44px tall on touch screens): View (the card large, grown from its thumbnail, focus going back to the result after: `viewing`'s `from` and `returnTo`) and "Add to Tier ›" (the graphic's `targetNoun`), whose flyout, opened by clicking it (not on hover; a second click closes it), lists every Add to choice with its swatch (`addResultTo`).
