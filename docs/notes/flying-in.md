# Flying in

Part of TierBinder's notes, indexed in `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them matching the code in the same PR that changes it.

- A card added by clicking a search result (or its menu's Add to choices) or a featured pool flies in, unless motion is reduced: a copy of it leaves its thumbnail for its spot on the board or in the card tray, along a slight arc (`FLIGHT_ARC`, 15% of the distance at its middle, bowing toward where it lands: down to a lower spot, up to a higher one) on `--ease-flight` (ease in and out, no overshoot) over `--duration-flight` (500ms), a pool's `FLIGHT_STAGGER` (40ms) apart, its own card hidden until it lands (`arriving` in `Editor.tsx`, `CardNode`'s `hidden`). `flyIn` in `Editor.tsx` starts them; `ui/flights.ts` draws them in plain DOM over the page (`.flight`, above the toast), reading the board's place once a frame so they follow it as it rescales or scrolls, and landing them in one update a frame.
- A result's card whose spot is off screen appears there at once. A pool's cards instead scroll the board's foot into view first, where the workspace scrolls beside the sidebar (`scrollIntoView`, waiting for `scrollend` or `SCROLL_WAIT`, 800ms), then fly up to the tray from wherever their thumbnails now are; the scroll is skipped when the last new card's spot is wholly on screen, and a thumbnail no longer shown (its panel hidden, or replaced by a search) leaves its card to appear at once.
- They always land: a timer lands any left when frames stop (a hidden tab, where the copies stay until it draws again, unseen), exports land them first, and so does leaving the page. A copy shows what its thumbnail loaded (the proxy, if the CDN failed); a pool's cards past the preview leave from the preview's thumbnails again in turn (the 7th from the 1st, and so on), so a big pool goes out in waves across the row, back the same way when removed, each showing its own picture. A copy only shows once it leaves (`showWaiting` keeps ones flying back out visible in their tray spots), so cards waiting their turn don't pile up. Their pictures start loading when the pointer or focus reaches the pool (`preloadPool`). Dragging a result and bulk import don't fly. Under reduced motion nothing flies, but a pool still scrolls the tray into view, at once.
