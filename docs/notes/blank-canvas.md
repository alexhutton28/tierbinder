# Blank canvas

Part of TierBinder's notes, indexed in `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them matching the code in the same PR that changes it.

- Cards placed anywhere: each item is a `CardItem` with its top-left `x`, `y`, kept wholly on the board (a narrower shape moves them back onto it, as a new card size does: `setCardShape`; ink past the new edge is clipped, and shows again on a wider shape). Later cards draw on top, and a moved card goes on top.
- Cards added from search or import fill a grid of as many spots as fit at the card size (10 × 4 at the default), by how many cards there are, starting over once full; a dropped result is centered on the pointer. Import groups mean nothing here, and cards before the first `#` land on the canvas too (`ungroupedImport: 'board'`), not in the card tray.
- Ink is clipped to the board, so it never draws over the card tray.
- Settings: `cardWidth` (Card Size, 40–400px wide, and never wider than the board, which a narrow shape's is: `setCardShape` caps it; the field applies once a typed number is in range, and leaving it takes a number out of range to the nearest limit), `topHalf` (Top Halves Only), `background` (white by default), and `border` for split cards' divider and stroke. Changing the size or top halves moves cards that would hang off the board back onto it.
- Tools (`BlankToolbar.tsx`, under the board): Move (the default: drag and click cards), Pen (color, size; default near-black `#1b1b1b`, 12px) and Eraser (size; default 24px). Pen and eraser settings are saved with the board.
- Ink: `strokes` (pen or eraser, with Konva `Line` points rounded to 0.1px) on their own layer above the cards. Eraser strokes use `destination-out`, so they remove only ink, never cards or the background. While drawing, a transparent rect on the ink layer takes every press (cards don't move or open menus), the stroke stays in component state until the pointer is released anywhere, and a click leaves a dot (the point is doubled). The pen shows a crosshair; the eraser hides the pointer and draws its size as a circle in an editor-only layer. Clear Board's choices clear the ink too.
- `CardNode` draws every card at 100×140 and scales it (`width`), so borders and corners scale too; `topHalf` clips to the top half. Split cards always show both halves, at full height, since their top half would hide the bottom card. `cardBox` gives the box a card takes, which the canvas reports in `layout` for the selection outline and drop hit-testing.
