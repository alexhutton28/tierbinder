# Card tray

Part of TierBinder's notes, indexed in `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them matching the code in the same PR that changes it.

- An optional holding area under the board, for every graphic, each board keeping its own (`tray`: `enabled`, `color`, `items`). Turned on in Customize (Card Tray's heading is its toggle, with "Tray Color" under it); turned off, its cards stay saved and come back. It shows at every width: on phones and tablets it's under the board, scaled with it, both staying on screen above the sidebar.
- Drawn in the same stage as the board, `TRAY_GAP` (16 on-screen px) under it, from `b8` width up always a 16:9 board's width (`TRAY_WIDTH`, 1200) whatever the board's shape, and under it (`rowTray`) the workspace's whole width at the board's scale (`shownTrayWidth`, from `trayRoom`; the scale there is fitted to the board alone, its one row scrolling sideways, so a narrow board isn't drawn smaller to fit a wider tray, and the tray still spans the page), and as tall as its cards and 15px padding (`trayHeight`: 170 for a full-size row, about as tall as a tier), with `TRAY_RADIUS` (8 on-screen px) corners. The stage grows to hold it, and the board's scale fits both. Turning it on or off (by any means on the same board: the toggle, Hide Card Tray, a card or pool added to it, undo) slides it: `useReveal` (`ui/useReveal.ts`) eases a value from 0 to 1 and back on `--ease-slide` over `--duration-move`, a frame at a time, with a timer that finishes it if frames stop; the stage's width (from the board's to the tray's), its height, the gap and the scale follow it (the scale measured in a layout effect, so no frame paints at the last one's), the tray centered on the stage as it widens (`trayLeft`), so the board rescales smoothly, the tray is revealed as the stage grows under it (clipped by the stage), and its layer fades over the first or last `--duration-fade` of it. A leaving tray is still drawn (`trayDrawn`) but takes no presses and isn't in `placements`. A board type switch, and reduced motion, snap instead; an export finishes it first (`trayReveal.finish`). Customize's rows are scrolled into view once it has slid in (`revealBelow`, after `--duration-move`). The easing and duration readers are `readEasing` and `readDuration` in `ui/motion.ts`, shared with the flights.
- A narrower board is centered over the tray: the stage is as wide as the tray and shifted by `boardLeft` (the Stage's `x`), so the board's own corner stays x = 0 for everything laid out on it, the tray's group starts at a negative x (`CardTray`'s `left`), tray placements are in the board's terms, and points go to the tray's through `inTray`. What's measured from the stage's box on screen adds `boardLeft` (flights' landing spots, View's origin, a dropped result's point). From `b8` up, while the tray shows, a narrow board is drawn smaller so the tray fits; hidden, the board has its full size. The tray never gets more than its two rows, and a card dragged up onto the board grows to the board's card size as it always has.
- Under `b8`, and in a side by side window under `b8` width (a phone turned sideways: `rowTray`, from `pastB8`, `b8Query`), so wherever the workspace is too narrow for a 1200-wide tray (`oneRowTray` in `Editor.tsx`), on screen only, the tray is one row of cards however many it holds, full size and 170 tall with the sidebar open, bigger while it's folded (see below) (`layoutTray`, `trayHeight`, `trayCardWidth` and `trayDropIndex` take `oneRow`), scrolled sideways when they overhang it (`trayRowWidth`; its card width is `oneRowTray`, `FOLDED_TRAY_SCALE` (1.3) times full size while the sidebar is folded, growing with the fold's slide, the board giving up a little height for it; bigger where a board limited by the width (Wide) leaves height under it, filling it (`trayFill`, measured in the fit: `rowCardWidthFor`) but never so big that fewer than `FOLDED_TRAY_VISIBLE` (3) show across; and never more than `FOLDED_TRAY_MAX` (100) CSS pixels wide on screen, nor smaller than full size), a shade fading in inside each edge with cards past it, holding a round Petrel 50 arrow that scrolls three quarters of the tray's width that way on `--duration-move` when tapped or clicked (a 44px target; `EdgeShade` in `CardTray.tsx`, named `editor`; a pointer shortcut drawn in Konva, out of reach of keyboards and screen readers, as the tray's swipe already is): `trayPan` in design pixels, kept within the overhang, applied to `trayPlacements` and `inTray` (so drops, the outline, menus and flights' landing spots follow it), and set to the row's end when a card is added at the end on the same board, or moved to the end by clicking the tray, so it lands in view (a pool's earlier cards can land out of view). A pan is applied once a frame. `CardTray` clips its cards to its box (`trayClip`) and scrolls them (`trayCards`); the export takes both off and lays them out in its two rows as before. On touch screens its cards don't start their own drags (`CardNode`'s `draggable`): a touch that moves `TOUCH_SLOP` (`ui/pointer.ts`) sideways pans the tray, and up or down starts the card's drag (`startDrag`), as does a pen or mouse moving that far in any direction; a tap that panned doesn't open a menu or pick the tray. A wheel's sideways scroll (or Shift and the wheel) pans it too. A selected card scrolled out of view keeps its outline outside the tray.
- Up to 48 cards (`MAX_TRAY_CARDS`), each row filled left to right before the next (from `b8` up, and in the image): up to 10 at full size in one row; then shrinking to fit it, down to 75% (`MIN_ROW_SHARE`, 14 cards); then a second row at 75% (up to 28); then both rows shrinking to fit (48 are two rows of 24). The tray's height follows (137 at 14 cards, 248 for two rows at 75%), so the board rescales as cards come and go, and the export's height changes with it.
- A card dragged between the board and the tray grows or shrinks (300ms, easing out) to the size where its center is: the board's (`cardWidth` on the `GraphicType`, else `CARD_WIDTH`) or the tray's with one more card (`trayCardWidth`), around the pointer, the spot it was picked up by staying under it (`CardNode`'s `dragWidth`, given to the graphics as `cardDragWidth`). The selection outline takes the card's size as it changes, and the drop is reported at the laid-out size around the card's center.
- It works like a tier: "Card Tray" is the last Add to choice (with its color as the swatch, outlined), even while the tray is off: adding a card to it turns it on (`withTray` sets `enabled`); clicking its empty space picks it (moving a selected card to its end, as a tier does), results dropped on it go in by position, and cards drag between it and the board (into the tray by their center; out of it, the graphic's `dropCard` at the card's center) and reorder within it. While a card is dragged, `CardNode` moves it alone into the `drag` group at the bottom of the editor's layer (above the board and the tray, below the outline) and back into its own layer and place on the drop, redrawing the layer it left each time (Konva doesn't); a card removed mid-drag (Delete, or the tray turned off) ignores its drop. A board card dragged over a full tray keeps its size. A result dropped on a tray card offers "Add to Card Tray". The card menu opens upward from tray cards, at the board's foot. While the pen or eraser is active the tray takes no presses. On a tier list (`ungroupedImport: 'tray'` on the `GraphicType`), bulk import puts the cards before its first `#` line in it, in order, as many as fit (adding any turns the tray on: `importTrayCards`). Its toast says how many cards went where ("Added 5 Cards to the Card Tray"), then any problems. The import is placed on the board as it is once the lookups finish (`docRef`). Clear Board's Clear All empties it if it shows, keeping it on; a tray turned off keeps its cards. The tray alone empties (keeping it on, deselecting a tray card, with a toast saying how many went: `clearTray`) from Clear Board's "Clear Card Tray" in Customize, from Clear Card Tray at the foot of a tray card's menu, or by right-clicking the tray's empty space: the card menu opens there as the tray's own (`TRAY_MENU`) with Hide Card Tray (turning it off, as unticking it in Customize does: `showTray`) and, while it holds cards, Clear Card Tray. Clear Card Tray, in either menu, is a row with a chevron that opens a flyout holding "Clear N Cards", as the search result menu's Add to row does (`toggleTrayFlyout`; the card menu's `flyout` is the count, so the label holds while it folds; `shownTrayFlyout`): on a click, Enter or the right arrow, not on hover, focusing that item; a second click closes it, and Escape or the left arrow in it goes back to the row (the card menu's arrows and Home/End move through its items, as the result menu's do: `onCardMenuKey`). It opens to the right, or the left when it wouldn't fit in the window (`data-side`, folding toward the row); the card menu doesn't clip it (`.cardMenu`), and only the menu it opened in shows it (`shownTrayFlyout`, by the menu's key). Its count follows cards that come or go meanwhile, it closes if its row goes, and once the tray is cleared or hidden the menu goes at once instead of folding, focus going to the board. On touch screens the card menu's rows are 44px tall, as the result menu's are. Hide Card Tray toasts "Card Tray Hidden; Show It Again in Customize". The tray's own menu opens on a right-click or, on touch screens, a long press on its space (held `LONG_PRESS` moving less than `TOUCH_SLOP`, as a card's: `startTrayPress` in `CardTray.tsx`; the tap it ends with doesn't pick the tray, and Android's own contextmenu for it is ignored); from the keyboard, a tray card's menu or Customize's button clears it. Removing several cards at once is [#41](https://github.com/alexhutton28/tierbinder/issues/41).
- Empty, it shows Feather's info icon (drawn in Konva, as tall as the text, 4 on-screen px before it) and "Send Cards to Card Tray to Hold Them" centered together in its middle (`TrayHint` in `CardTray.tsx`, `TRAY_HINT`), 14 on-screen px of Figtree, white at 45% or Petrel 950 at 60%, (whichever reads on the tray, `darkReadsBetter` in `color.ts`), named `editor` so exports leave it out. Once a card has been in a tray it isn't shown again for the tab's session (`tierbinder-tray-hint-seen` in `sessionStorage`, so a reload keeps it hidden).
- Save & Share's Include Card Tray in Export toggle (off by default, not saved) exports it flush and square under the board, at the board's width: on a 16:9 board 2400 wide and 1350 plus twice the tray's height tall (1690 with up to 10 cards); on a narrower one its cards are laid out again at that width, in the same two rows at most, so smaller (`layoutTray(tray, width)`), and the image stays one rectangle 1080 wide. The export draws the stage at scale 1, unshifted, at the shape's `exportScale`, moving the `tray` group under the board, resizing and squaring its `trayBox` and moving its cards just while drawing, all put back before the screen next draws (`renderBlob`'s `set` and `restore`).
