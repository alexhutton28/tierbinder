# Share links

Part of TierBinder's notes, indexed in `CLAUDE.md`. Keep them matching the code in the same PR that changes it.

- Save & Share's buttons depend on the device (`shareSheet`, checked once: a coarse pointer and `navigator.canShare` with a PNG file). Phones and tablets that can share get Share Image (primary: the PNG to the system's share sheet, whose Save Image puts it in Photos), Copy Image and Share Board Link (there's no download button there: iOS puts downloads in Files, not Photos) (the link to the share sheet); everything else gets Download (primary), Copy Image and Copy Board Link. Copy Image hands the clipboard the image as a promise in a `ClipboardItem` made inside the click, since Safari refuses a write made after an await. Each button has a Feather icon (`.buttonIcon` in `Editor.module.css`, by `data-icon`). iOS only opens the share sheet straight after a tap, and drawing the image can take longer, so `shareThrough` keeps what it made for the board as it is (`readyShare`, by graphic and Include Card Tray in Export): when the sheet is refused (`NotAllowedError`) a toast says "Sharing Didn't Open; Try Again", and the second tap shares it at once, with nothing awaited first. Closing the sheet without picking anything (`AbortError`) says nothing. The sheet isn't awaited by the buttons (WebKit's share promise sometimes never settles). The kept share is dropped when the board or the canvas font changes. The image is named `tierbinder-` and the graphic's kind (`tierbinder-tier-list.png`).
- They say what they're doing while it runs (`Working`: "Downloading", "Copying", "Sharing"), until the dots have hopped two whole rounds (`DOTS_SHOWN` of `DOTS_CYCLE`, 1s, in `ui/Working.tsx`), or the next whole round if it takes longer, so a quick one doesn't flicker and none stops mid-hop; one at a time (`whileWorking`, which starts its task at once so a clipboard write stays inside the click).
- Save & Share's Board Link section ("Opens an editable copy of this board.") has Copy Board Link (Share Board Link with a share sheet), which copies a link to the board showing, card tray included (`shareLink` in `share.ts`). Nothing is stored: the board's JSON, without ids or cards' `kind` (parsing gives them back), is deflated (`CompressionStream('deflate-raw')`) and written in base64url after `#board=1.` (a format version), in the fragment, which never reaches the server. A small board's link is about 600 characters and a full one a few thousand; Copying it says "Link Copied", whatever its length. A board with more than `MAX_SHARED_CARDS` (400, board and tray: `sharedCards`) isn't made into a link ("Too Many Cards to Share (400 at Most)"). The clipboard gets the link as a promise in a `ClipboardItem` made inside the click, since Safari refuses a write made after an await (`writeText` where there's no `ClipboardItem`). Short stored links are a planned follow-up.
- Opening one (on load, or pasted into an open tab: `hashchange`) reads it with `readShareHash`, unpacking at most `MAX_BOARD_BYTES` (200 KB), parsing it as a saved board is, and refusing one with more than `MAX_SHARED_CARDS` (a short link of repeated cards could otherwise hold a board too big to draw, which would then be saved). Of two reads at once (links pasted quickly), only the latest counts. A link that doesn't read toasts "Couldn't Open This Link". When this device's board of its type has nothing on it (no cards, ink or tray cards), it opens at once; otherwise a modal `<dialog>` asks ("Open Shared Tier List?": Keep Mine, or Open Shared Board in the primary color; Escape keeps theirs; keys stop at it, even with focus on the page's body, through a capture listener on the window as View has; it closes View first). Opening replaces that board and shows it (`openShared`, switching type first in an update of its own, since switching isn't an undo step, so the replacement is one): toast "Shared Board Opened" with Undo, and ⌘Z puts theirs back while the visit lasts. Answering sends focus to the board. Either way the hash is cleared (`history.replaceState`), so a reload doesn't ask again.
